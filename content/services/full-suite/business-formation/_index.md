    body: |
      Anyone can file an LLC. Online filing services do it in minutes, and the state does not check whether the choice makes sense for you. The expensive mistakes in business formation are almost never filing mistakes: they are tax mistakes. The wrong entity type, a missed S-corp election, a foreign-owned LLC that never heard of [Form 5472](/resources/smllc-5472/), or a company with no compliance calendar that finds out about penalties by mail.

      We approach formation as tax advisors first. Before anything gets filed, we look at how the entity will be taxed, who owns it, where it operates, and which elections and registrations should follow. Then we handle the practical setup end to end. Formations and registered agent service are fulfilled through a national registered agent partner, which lets us set up and support entities in all 50 states without slowing anything down.

  - title: "How Formation Works, Start to Finish"
    body: |
      Every formation follows the same sequence, and you know where things stand at each step:

      1. **Entity type.** We start with your tax situation, not a form. We look at whether an LLC, S-corp, or C-corp fits how you earn and plan to grow. The popular answer is not always the right one, and we will tell you when it is not.
      2. **State filing and registered agent.** We prepare and submit the formation documents and put registered agent service in place through our partner network, in any state.
      3. **EIN.** We obtain the EIN so the business can open bank accounts, hire, and file. That includes foreign owners without an SSN, where the [Form SS-4](/resources/ein-without-ssn-foreign-owners/) process works differently.
      4. **Tax elections.** If an S-corp or C-corp election would save you money, we prepare it, file it, and calendar the deadline so the election actually takes effect.
      5. **Compliance calendar.** Annual reports, renewals, and filing deadlines go on a calendar we track. You should never learn about a deadline from a penalty notice.
  - title: "Who This Is For"
    body: |
      **Foreign owners starting a U.S. business.** You do not need an SSN to own a U.S. company, but the setup runs differently: the EIN application takes a different path, an ITIN via [Form W-7](/resources/itin-application-form-w-7/) may be needed if you have a personal U.S. filing requirement, and foreign ownership brings reporting obligations that most filing services never mention. We build all of that into the setup.

      **U.S. founders starting fresh.** We get the entity, EIN, and elections right from day one and wire the business into payroll and bookkeeping from the start, so the first tax season is a filing, not a reconstruction project.

      **Existing businesses with admin piling up.** Renewals that lapsed, annual reports that never got filed, a registered agent you want to switch, or an entity that needs cleanup before it can move forward. We catch the entity up and put it on a calendar so it stays current.
  - title: "Ongoing Support After Setup"
    body: |
      We do not treat setup as a one-time filing and disappear. We stay involved with renewals, annual reports, registered agent coordination, and the administrative tasks that keep the business current. You will know what is due, when it is due, and what it costs before we do the work. That support prevents missed deadlines and reduces the cleanup that would otherwise show up at tax season.
