## Location Pages

### What's Working vs. What's Not

The locations ranking well (Fort Myers pos 5.3, Port St. Lucie pos 4.5, Cape Coral pos 17) share a pattern: lower competition and specific local queries. The ones buried (Miami pos 60, Doral pos 46, Coral Gables pos 49) are competing with large directories (Yelp, TaxBuzz, Thumbtack) and well-established local firms on generic "CPA near me" terms.

**Key insight for all location pages:** Vantis is not a generic local CPA — it's an international tax specialist. Every location page should lead with that angle, not try to out-compete generic "CPA [city]" searches. "International tax CPA in Miami" and "CPA for Latin American business owners in Doral" are far more winnable than "Miami CPA."

**What every location page needs:**
- A specific niche angle in the H1 and title (not just "[City] CPA")
- Named neighborhoods and submarkets (the Miami page does this well)
- The client types Vantis actually serves in that city
- Local context: what makes this market unique tax-wise
- Link from the /locations/ hub and from relevant resource articles
