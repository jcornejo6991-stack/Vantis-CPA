## Page 1 — /resources/section-179-vehicle-deduction/
**Current: 90 impressions, position 67**

This page ranks poorly because it's too thin compared to what's out there. The query data shows heavy demand around vehicle weight — people searching "6000 lb vehicle," "vehicles over 6000 pounds," "section 179 vehicle weight," "GVWR 6000," and dozens of similar variations. The fix is depth, not a new angle.

Add the following sections (slot them into the existing article structure naturally — don't just append a wall of text):

**1. Qualifying Vehicle List**
A table of common vehicles that exceed 6,000 lbs GVWR, organized by category (SUVs, trucks, vans). Include make, model, approximate GVWR, and whether they typically qualify. Note that GVWR must be verified on the door jamb sticker, not assumed from the vehicle class.

**2. Section 179 vs Bonus Depreciation**
Explain when to use each, how they interact, and whether you can use both on the same vehicle. Cover the $28,900 luxury auto cap for passenger vehicles and why heavy vehicles avoid it.

**3. S-Corp vs Sole Proprietor Treatment**
How vehicle deductions work differently depending on entity structure. S-corp owners who use a personal vehicle for business need an accountable plan reimbursement — they can't deduct directly on the 1120-S.

**4. Business-Use Percentage**
How to calculate and document the business-use percentage. What records the IRS expects. What happens if business use drops below 50% in a later year.

**5. Recapture Rules**
What happens if you sell the vehicle or business use drops. How depreciation recapture is taxed as ordinary income.

**6. Common Mistakes**
Leased vehicles, vehicles used for commuting, vehicles titled in the owner's name vs the business name.
