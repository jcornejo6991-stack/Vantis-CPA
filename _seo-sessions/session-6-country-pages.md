## Pages to Build

### 1. /resources/us-tax-for-venezuelans/

**Target queries:** "us tax for venezuelans," "venezuelan expat us taxes," "venezuela us tax"

**Country-specific content to include:**
- Venezuela has no tax treaty with the US — all income is taxable without treaty relief
- Large Venezuelan diaspora in South Florida (Miami, Doral) with US businesses and real estate
- Common situation: Venezuelan nationals with US LLCs, US real estate, or US bank accounts
- FBAR and FATCA for Venezuelan accounts (although many Venezuelans have moved funds to US)
- Pre-immigration planning for Venezuelans relocating to the US
- Venezuelan currency controls and how US tax treats income earned in Venezuela

**Meta title:** US Tax for Venezuelan Nationals: What You Need to Know | Vantis CPA
**Meta description:** Venezuelan nationals living in the US or investing here face specific filing obligations. Here's what triggers US tax, FBAR reporting, and what to do before relocating.
