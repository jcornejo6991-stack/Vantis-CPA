- Whether the S-corp can deduct contributions on the 1120-S (yes, as a compensation expense)
- Roth Solo 401k option for S-corp owners — no income limits
- When SEP-IRA still makes sense even for S-corp owners (late setup, simplicity)
- Internal links to: /resources/solo-401k-vs-sep-ira/, /resources/s-corp-reasonable-salary/, /resources/s-corp-election/
